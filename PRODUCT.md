# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SnapTrade Personal users who want a fast, glanceable view of their connected brokerage portfolio from the macOS menu bar.

## Product Purpose

Show current portfolio value, positions, connection health, and data freshness without requiring users to open a full dashboard. Success means users can trust the displayed value and quickly recognize when brokerage-reported and independently calculated data disagree.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and native to macOS.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative finance-dashboard styling, unnecessary charts, crowded controls, and interactions that feel foreign to macOS.

## Design Principles

- Make data provenance and freshness explicit.
- Prefer trustworthy, independently verifiable values over false precision.
- Keep the portfolio glanceable and compact.
- Preserve familiar macOS behavior and visual hierarchy.
- Surface discrepancies without making normal market movement feel alarming.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use standard macOS controls, semantic labels, system typography, sufficient contrast, and indicators that do not rely on color alone.
